How Payouts Work

When your items are sold as part of a lot, funds are automatically distributed upon successful delivery to the buyer. Your share is calculated proportionally based on your item's value:

Payout =
(Item Price / Lot Starting Price)
× (Winning Bid - Commission)

Admin commission is 10% (or 2% for Real Estate).
